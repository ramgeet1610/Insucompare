## Estimates only — not a guarantee

All costs shown by this tool are **estimates**, not quotes or guarantees. In particular:

- **Premium and benefit data** is sourced from OPM's published Public Use Files for the relevant plan year and is intended to reflect official figures, but may contain errors, omissions, or become outdated, particularly if you use this tool outside the plan year it was built for.
- **Drug tier and pharmacy cost estimates** are based on a limited, independently researched dataset covering a small number of medications and insurance carriers. Most plans and most medications are **not** covered by this data, and will be clearly marked as "unknown" rather than estimated. Where a tier or cost is shown, it reflects research conducted at a point in time and may not reflect your specific plan's current formulary, prior authorization requirements, step therapy rules, or quantity limits.
- **Visit cost estimates** for services billed as coinsurance (a percentage of an "allowed amount") use a placeholder assumed dollar value, because actual allowed amounts vary by provider, location, and specific service. These are rough approximations, not real prices.
- **Network and provider information** is limited to a general classification of each plan's network breadth (for example, whether a plan is nationwide, regional, or a local HMO). This tool does **not** verify whether any specific doctor, hospital, or provider participates in any plan's network. Always confirm directly with the plan or provider.
