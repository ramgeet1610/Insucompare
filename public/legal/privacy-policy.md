# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 2, 2026**

## The short version

This tool does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any of your personal information — including your ZIP code, health conditions, or medications. Everything you type into the comparison tool runs entirely on your own device. We never see it.

## What this tool does

InsuCompare helps you estimate and compare annual costs across Federal Employee Health Benefits (FEHB) medical plans, FEDVIP dental plans, and FEDVIP vision plans, using information you provide and publicly available data published by the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM) and individual insurance carriers.

## What we collect

**Nothing you type into the comparison tool.** Specifically:

- We do not operate a server that receives, logs, or stores the ZIP code, enrollment type, usage level, or medication information you enter into the comparison tool.
- We do not create accounts, require sign-in, or retain any record of your comparison inputs.
- All comparison calculations happen locally in your browser. When you close this page or navigate away, nothing you entered into the comparison tool persists anywhere — not on our systems, because we don't have any that store it.
- We do not use advertising pixels, advertising networks, or any cross-site tracking.

**We do count visits.** This site uses Vercel Web Analytics so we can see how many people use the tool and which parts of it they use. It records the page you viewed, your country, your device type, operating system and browser, and the site that referred you, together with an identifier used to count unique visitors. It does not set a cookie, and it is not used to follow you to other websites.

Vercel Web Analytics never receives anything you type into the comparison tool. Your ZIP code, enrollment type, expected usage and medications are not sent to it, because they are never sent anywhere at all. See Vercel's privacy policy at https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Keeping your health information out of our hands is a deliberate design choice, not an oversight: the comparison tool was built specifically to avoid collecting consumer health data, including information about medications, because we believe that information is sensitive and should not be collected at all if it doesn't need to be. Counting page views does not require any of it, so we don't collect any of it.

## Third-party services this tool relies on

To function, this site loads a small number of external resources. These are services run by other companies, and their own privacy practices apply to the limited technical data (such as your IP address, as part of normal internet routing) that any web request necessarily involves:

- **Vercel** — hosts the website files and provides the Web Analytics described above. Vercel automatically records standard request logs (IP address, timestamp, requested URL, user agent) for operational and security purposes. See their privacy policy at https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.
- **Google Fonts** — loads the typefaces used on this page. Loading a font from Google necessarily discloses your IP address to Google. See their privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
- **ZIP code reference data** — provided by SimpleMaps (simplemaps.com/data/us-zips). This data is built into the comparison tool itself and does not involve any data being sent to SimpleMaps when you use this tool.

We do not control these third parties' practices beyond what's necessary to load this page and count visits to it.

## Plan and pricing data sources

The plan, premium, and benefit data used in the comparison tool comes from the U.S. Office of Personnel Management's (OPM) published Public Use Files, which are public government data, not information about you. Drug tier and network information is independently researched from carrier-published sources, also not specific to you.

## Children's privacy

This tool is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

## Changes to this policy

If we ever change how this tool works — for example, if we add features that require collecting information beyond what's described here — we will update this policy first and clearly describe what changes and why, before any such feature goes live.
